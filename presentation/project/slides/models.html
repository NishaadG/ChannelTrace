Attribution models

Seven ways to split the credit for one sale

ModelTypeHow credit is assigned
First touchRuleAll credit to the first interaction
Last touchRuleAll credit to the final interaction (what most platforms report)
LinearRuleSplit equally across every interaction
Time decayRuleMore credit closer to the purchase (7-day half-life)
Position basedRule40% first, 40% last, 20% shared in the middle
Markov chainData-drivenHow far conversions fall if the channel is removed (Anderl et al., 2016)
Shapley valueData-drivenAverage marginal contribution across channel combinations (Shao and Li, 2011)

No model is objectively correct, because no dataset records which ad truly caused a sale. That is why we compare all seven and measure how much a channel's apparent value depends on the rule. The two data-driven models learn from both converting and non-converting journeys.
