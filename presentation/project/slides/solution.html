Proposed solution

Three connected parts

Research study

Journey, attribution and conversion analysis on public datasets, written up as a paper with original results.

Plug-and-play dashboard

Upload a Google Ads, Meta Ads or journey CSV. The format is detected and the dashboard builds itself.

AI marketing assistant

Plain-language answers and suggested actions, grounded only in the computed results.

Unlike a reporting dashboard, it answers research questions, works on any business's own data and compares seven attribution models instead of showing one number.
